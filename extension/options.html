SPENDSHIELD LIVE

Your browser. Your boundaries.

Connect your local SpendShield app, then choose which insights and safeguards you want.

1. Connect your app

Copy this extension ID into SpendShield → My Plan → SpendShield Live, then save there.

Use the address shown by your local launcher. The isolated judge demo uses port 8002. Hosted accounts are not supported by this extension yet.

2. Choose your safeguards

Off by default. When off, Gemini page interpretation begins only when you open a purchase check. Gemini also needs to be enabled in the app.

Privacy & control

SpendShield only analyzes purchase and financial context needed to provide spending insights. It checks product metadata, headings and price labels locally. It never reads form values, credentials or payment fields, and never sends full HTML. URL query strings and fragments are discarded.

Relevant snippets may be sent to your local backend and, with Gemini enabled, Google. AI interprets; Python calculates. No Gemini key is stored in the extension.

The browser permission to read pages enables detection. Ordinary pages are not uploaded. You can restrict site access in Chrome. Shield Mode is a voluntary page cover, not a tamper-proof block or treatment. You can disable it here, or disable the extension in Chrome even while offline.
